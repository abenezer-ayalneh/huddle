# Signal Handoff legal dossiers

**Mode:** Read

**Audience and job:** People evaluating a Huddle deployment's privacy terms or responsibilities need to find an exact section, compare the paired documents, and read long-form policy text without losing their place.

**Direction:** The legal pages inherit Signal Handoff's warm cream/dark chocolate themes, Archivo hierarchy, purple authority, yellow signal routes, and floating navigation. Their memorable moment is an asymmetric dossier masthead that hands into a persistent numbered contents rail and an unbroken reading column.

**Constraints:** Preserve every legal disclosure, metadata value, canonical route, anchor ID, and existing operator-template edit. The shared `huddle-theme` preference controls both routes. Desktop contents stay sticky; mobile contents collapse natively and show the active section. No marketing claims, fabricated proof, or imagery.
